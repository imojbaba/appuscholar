# Parity port: deviation register

One row per deliberate difference from v1: the flag id, what differs, why, who approved it and the test that pins the new behaviour. Nothing deviates without a row (spec `2026-10-17-parity-port-design.md`, §5.6). The two rules: "numbers never deviate without OJ; copy never deviates without Dr. Sharma".

- **Ids.** An audit flag keeps its `F` number from §4 of `PORT-AUDIT.md`, in `appuscholar-blueprint/` (a sibling folder outside this repo): `F5`. A letter suffix (`F23a`) marks a sub-finding of an audit flag that the plan introduces. A new row is `D-W<wave>-<n>`, with `n` counting up within the wave.
- **Statuses.**
    - `proposed`: awaits OJ for numbers, Dr. Sharma for copy.
    - `approved`: accepted; the approver and date are in the row.
    - `rejected`: declined; no change is made.
    - `noted`: asks for no decision — it changes no output, or the difference was decided in an earlier phase and is recorded here so it is not rediscovered.
- **Adding a row.** Every task that deviates appends a row at the bottom as `proposed` or `noted`, with all eight cells filled (`—` where there is nothing yet) and no `|` inside a cell (write "or", or a comma). A golden mismatch is a port bug or a row, never a hand-edited fixture.
- **Lines and dates.** v1 line numbers are those of `index.html` build 2026.10.13a (13,699 lines). The date in "approver and date" is the day the approval was recorded here (YYYY-MM-DD).
