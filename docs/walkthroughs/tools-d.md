# Tools D walk-through: Writing's My style and Words tabs

Definition of done for session D of the stages 3 to 6 tools plan (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-21-stages-3-6-tools.md`): in the Writing place, **My style** (learn your voice from your own writing, check it, use it in suggestions) and **Words** (a dictionary with a word bank, and a grammar check by rules and by AI) are built and work with the document shown beside them. A student meets each of them on the live site. Tests passing is necessary, not sufficient.
Walked by OJ, in Chrome, on the live site. The same behaviour runs automatically in `apps/web/e2e/tools-d.spec.ts` (rows 1 to 6, with the AI and the dictionary answered by recordings); row 0 and the live site, with a real AI key and the real dictionary, are this sheet's alone. Keep the two in step.

**Site:** https://appuscholar-web.vercel.app
**You need:**
- **An account with a project** whose idea is saved (the walk account from wave B1 will do), signed in, with its passphrase. Your AI key must be saved and the vault unlocked: type the passphrase when asked, and do not reload the page between rows (a reload locks the vault again and these tabs then say their data is locked).
- **A paragraph of your own writing**, at least 80 words, written by you without AI (a paragraph from an old essay will do). Keep it in a note to paste into the document.
- **A short text with two slips**: `Please return back the informations to the office before Friday.`
- **A text with an email address in it**: `The students was happy with the results. Send the form to asha@uni.ac.in before the deadline closes.`
- **A desktop browser** at about 1440 px wide, and the browser's device mode at 375 px wide (row 6).

**Record:** fill the last column for each row with `VERIFIED` or `FAILED` and the date (`YYYY-MM-DD`). Anything FAILED is fixed and re-run before D is called done. Build under test: open **Help** in the header and read the "Version ..." line at its top; write it here: `________`

## Things Dr. Sharma should look at

These are the places where v2 differs from v1 (the register is `docs/superpowers/specs/parity-deviations.md`; each row there is `proposed` and awaits her review):

- **D-S2-85** My style moves into a tab: no emoji, only the toasts "Saved" and "Inserted", an inline question for erase instead of a browser pop-up, the AI's profile labelled, English wording rewritten in plain words.
- **D-S2-86** The style data (samples as features only, profile, declaration, checks) is kept in the project's sealed data, not in the browser; a locked or unreadable store says so; the 101st sample is told it was not kept.
- **D-S2-87** "Show my voice" is off while "Use my style in suggestions" is off (v1 sent a plain paraphrase then).
- **D-S2-88** The "pasted" mark clears when the paraphrase box is emptied, so "Clear the box and type it" can work; "Remove sample" is new.
- **D-S2-89** "Learn from my writing" reads only the student's own words, not text the AI inserted into the document.
- **D-S2-90** The dictionary's memory is the page's only (not saved on disk); the dictionary service is named in the page's security rules; fixed error sentences go through the dictionary of words.
- **D-S2-91** The word bank, the spelling choice and the pattern counts live in the project's sealed data; the 501st word is told, not silently dropped; the list says it shows the newest 60.
- **D-S2-92** Words and grammar move into a tab: no emoji, only the "Saved" toast, a retry for the Hindi meaning after an AI error, AI cards labelled, "Remove word", rule texts stay in English.
- **D-S2-93** Apply goes through the editor so Ctrl+Z undoes it, and a card whose words are gone stays with a plain line; an AI fix is typed in as the student's own edit, not marked as AI text.
