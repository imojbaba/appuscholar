# Tools A walk-through: Design and ethics

Definition of done for session A of the stages 3 to 6 tools plan (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-21-stages-3-6-tools.md`): the Design and ethics place is built. Its eight tabs (**Overview**, **Pathway**, **Protocol**, **Ethics and consent**, **Registration**, **Investigator**, **Budget**, **AI-use statement**) each do their job, keep your work between visits, and say plainly that only your committee decides what applies and that nothing is ever sent anywhere for you. A student meets all of it on the live site. Tests passing is necessary, not sufficient.
Walked by OJ, in Chrome, on the live site. The same behaviour runs automatically in `apps/web/e2e/tools-a.spec.ts` (rows 1 to 8, with the AI-use log answered by a fixed list); rows 0, 9, 10 and 11 and the live site are this sheet's alone. Keep the two in step.

**Site:** https://appuscholar-web.vercel.app
**You need:**
- **An account with a project** whose idea is saved (the walk account from wave B1 will do), signed in, with its passphrase if the vault is on.
- **A desktop browser** at about 1440 px wide, and the browser's device mode at 375 px wide (row 8).
- **A few AI requests already made in that project** (for example two rewrites and one comment from Writing), so the AI-use log has rows (row 6). If you have none, make them first.
- **A Hindi check** needs nothing more than the **हि** button at the top right (row 9).

**Record:** fill the last column for each row with `VERIFIED` or `FAILED` and the date (`YYYY-MM-DD`). Anything FAILED is fixed and re-run before A is called done. Build under test: open **Help** in the header and read the "Version ..." line at its top; write it here: `________`
