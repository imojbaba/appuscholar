# Tools B walk-through: Collection and analysis (statistics)

Definition of done for session B of the stages 3 to 6 tools plan (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-21-stages-3-6-tools.md`): the Collection and analysis place has five working tabs, **Statistics**, **Sample size**, **Charts**, **Check numbers** and **Scales**, and a student can paste a table, mind the personal details in it, run a test, put the sentence in the document, ask for it to be explained, check it later against changed data, choose a test with the guide, work out a sample size, draw a figure and score a scale. Tests passing is necessary, not sufficient: a student has to walk it on the live site.
Walked by OJ, in Chrome, on the live site, with a passphrase account. The same behaviour runs automatically in `apps/web/e2e/tools-b.spec.ts` (the rows marked "e2e step N" below carry that test's step number); the live site, the downloads, the look of the pictures and the rows not marked are this sheet's alone. Keep the two in step.

**Site:** https://appuscholar-web.vercel.app
**You need:**
- **An account with a project** whose idea is saved, signed in, with its passphrase (the walk account from wave B1 will do), and an AI key connected (**Set up my key**) for row 5.

- **Chrome and Safari** for the picture download in row 10 and row 11 (see "Things only a person can judge").

## Things only a person can judge

These cannot be decided by a test, and the build is not signed off until OJ has looked at each:

1. **The quick chart's look.** It is a new picture (drawn by the app as an SVG), not the old chart library's. Does it read as a clear bar or line chart? Is the zero line obvious, are the labels readable at the document's width, are cut-off long labels acceptable (the label ends in "...")?
2. **Hindi draft wording.** Switch the language to Hindi and open each tab: **Statistics**, **Sample size**, **Charts**, **Check numbers**, **Scales**. Every Hindi line the five tabs add is a draft, marked DRAFT in the message files. Does each read as a student would say it?
3. **English-only core texts in Hindi mode.** In Hindi mode the sentences core writes (the APA sentence, the test names, the refusal texts, the guide's questions and answers, the sample size sentences and the scale items) stay in English, as in v1. Is that acceptable for now, or should any be translated before release?
4. **The PNG in Chrome and Safari.** Insert a figure (Studio and quick chart), press **Download PNG**, and open the file in both browsers; also zoom in the document. Is it at twice the size and not blurry, with the text and axis intact, and no black background on a transparent picture?
5. **Chart Studio's "Axis from" warning (rule 14).** With **Axis from** above 0 on a bar chart the amber warning appears and the chart is still drawn. Is that the right balance (warn, do not block), and is the wording plain enough?
6. **The name-heading scan's limit.** The personal-data scan reads headings and the shape of values, and skips a column headed "name" with few different values (a group label). A column of names headed `participant` with repeated values, or names typed inside answers, is not flagged. Try a few honest-looking tables of your own and decide whether the note under the box says enough.
