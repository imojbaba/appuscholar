# Wave B1 walk-through: the frame, stage states and locked titles

Definition of done for slice B1 of wave B (the routes and the frame, first run, the project's home with its six stage states, the project list, the places with Literature's four tabs, the document beside any stage, one export place, a place in its own window, the phone layout, titles sealed in the vault): a student meets each of them on the live site. Tests passing is necessary, not sufficient.
The same behaviour runs automatically: `apps/web/e2e/stage2-idea-literature.spec.ts` walks rows 2 to 4, 6, 10 to 13 and 15 (steps 1, 2, 3 to 10, 11, 12, 12b and 13b, with the AI, OpenAlex, PubMed and Crossref answered from recorded responses; its final check reads every stage blob, the project's title and its documents' titles back from the api, the e2e's version of row 7); `phase3a-vault.spec.ts` walks rows 7 and 8 (steps 3, 4c and 4d); `frame-responsive.spec.ts` walks row 14; row 5's states are unit tests (`apps/web/test/stages.test.ts`, `firstRun.test.tsx`). This sheet is the human walk. Keep the two in step. `docs/walkthroughs/stage-2.md` is the full stage 2 journey; its rows 1 and 12 describe the frame, and its other rows still name the tools, which now sit in Literature's tabs (Find is Research, Reading list is the shelf and Analyse a paper, Compare is the shelf's table and the paper cards, Citations is the Citation Manager).

**Site:** https://appuscholar-web.vercel.app
**You need:**
- **A fresh throwaway account**: a new email address you can sign in with, so the terms, the welcome window and first run appear. Use it for nothing else.
- **Your Groq or OpenAI key** for the AI steps (rows 2 and 13's search, if you run one). A passphrase of at least 10 characters that you will remember: the walk types it several times. Keep the recovery sheet the setup shows.
- **The walk account from wave A** (row 9), with its passphrase: an account whose titles were written before B1, so they are still plaintext on the server.
- **The real OpenAlex, PubMed and Crossref.** Your browser calls them directly. They receive only what the app sends: search words, PubMed ids, OpenAlex ids and DOIs, and for a source without a DOI its title and first author's surname (row 13).
- **A desktop browser** at about 1440 px wide, allowed to open pop-up windows for this site (row 13), and a phone or the browser's device mode at 375 px wide (row 14).
- **For rows 7 and 9 only, a developer:** read access to the project's Neon database. No credentials belong on this sheet.

**Record:** fill the last column for each row with `VERIFIED` or `FAILED` and the date (`YYYY-MM-DD`). Anything FAILED is fixed and re-run before B1 is called done. Build under test: open **Help** in the header and read the "Version ..." line at its top; write it here: `________`
