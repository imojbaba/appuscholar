# Participants walk-through: the participant list and name matching

Definition of done for the participant list (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-participants-design.md`, branch `participants-register`): a student plans in **Design and ethics** how participants are identified; the plan flows into the consent form, the ethics statement and the protocol; the first tab of **Collection and analysis**, **Participants**, holds a list of the people in the study, sealed in the vault, each with one code for the whole project; Appuscholar finds exactly those names in pasted tables, in the project's documents and in AI requests, and swaps them for codes only when the student presses a button. Names that are not on the list are offered as suggestions and never acted on by default. This fixes two faults from the 2026-10-23 walk: **F1** (open answers in a table were taken for names and swapped for codes) and **O1** (the consent form had no parent's permission or young person's agreement). Tests passing is necessary, not sufficient: a person has to walk it on the live site.

Walked by OJ, in Chrome, on the live site, with a passphrase account. The same behaviour runs automatically in `apps/web/e2e/participants.spec.ts` (local servers, the AI answered in the browser, never a real AI call); the column "e2e step" says which step of that test repeats a row. The live site, the real AI, the look of the highlights, the Hindi, the phone and the rows marked "no" are this sheet's alone. Keep the two in step.

**What happens underneath, and why**
- **The plan** is three new fields (`idMode`, `record`, `recordOther`) on the Ethics tab's stored record (`design_ec`). Core builds the consent form, ethics statement and protocol from it (`packages/core/src/designPlan.ts`: `consentMarkdownV2`, `ethicsStatementV2`, `protocolMarkdownV2`, `ecMinorsNoteV2`), so plan and paperwork cannot drift apart. With the question unanswered the ethics statement and the protocol are v1's, word for word; the consent form still gets its two under-18 sections whenever the under-18 answer is yes or unsure (they fix O1), and the under-18 note always ends with the new sentence.
- **The list** is one stored blob of kind `participants` (`apps/web/lib/blobs/kinds/participants.ts`). The browser seals it with the vault key before it is sent; the API refuses an unsealed write of this kind (409, `apps/api/src/routes/blobWrite.ts`). It is the one exception to the rule that participant data is never stored (register D-S2-116), because the list is what ties a code to a person and cannot be rebuilt.
- **Matching** is one function set in `packages/core/src/participants.ts` (and `participantsTable.ts` for tables): the person's name and each spelling they wrote, any case, whole words only, Devanagari vowel signs counted as part of the word. A name that is also an everyday word is asked about one place at a time. It runs in four places: the Statistics box (`components/tools/stats/PdBox.tsx`), **Find names in my documents** (`FindNames.tsx`), the highlights in the open document (`DocumentLayer.tsx`, painted by the browser and never stored) and the check before an AI request (`lib/ai/callAI.ts`, `pdDialog.tsx`).
- **Why:** v1 guessed names from their shape and replaced whole columns of open answers (F1). The student now says who the participants are, once, and Appuscholar looks for those people.

**Not built, in words**
- Replacing names in **earlier saved versions** of a document or in the trash: not built (the tab says so after a replace; row 13).
- Turning one script into the other (Priya into प्रिया): not built, on purpose. The student lists each spelling.
- A "participant characteristics" paragraph (how many, the gender split, the mean age): not built; for Dr. Sharma to ask for.
- Deleting a withdrawn person's turns from a transcript: not built. Appuscholar flags the finds ("withdrew: remove their data"); removing the turns is the student's job.
- Highlights in a second window (**Open in a new window**): not built; they are painted in the writing window only.

**Record:** fill the last column for each row with `VERIFIED`, `SUPPORTED`, `BELIEVED` or `UNKNOWN` and the date (`YYYY-MM-DD`). `VERIFIED`: you did the steps on the live site and saw what the row expects. `SUPPORTED`: a test or a stand-in shows it, but you did not see the real thing (for example a scripted click). `BELIEVED`: argued from the code, not seen. `UNKNOWN`: not tried, or the cause is not known. If what you saw is not what the row expects, write `FAILED`, say in words what you saw, and the row is fixed and walked again before this is called done. ## Things only a person can judge

These cannot be decided by a test, and the build is not called done until OJ has looked at each (the ones about wording and layout go to Dr. Sharma after it is live, spec section 11):

1. **The tab's layout and columns.** Is the table what a student expects (Code, Name and other spellings, Joined, the Consent group, the About-them group), and is the amber "consent not complete" a warning and not a scolding? It never stops a save.
2. **The menu's fields and lines** in **Ethics and consent**: are the "who they are" and "about them" groups clear, are the one-line reasons right, is "Sensitive: your committee will ask why you need it" the right tone?
3. **The consent form's new sections and the minors note.** Would a 15-year-old understand "Young person's agreement"? Is the note's last sentence the wording Dr. Sharma wants?
4. **The highlight colours.** Are purple (a name on your list) and amber (ask, or not on your list) easy to tell apart, in the editor's light background, at the document's size? Does typing feel the same with the list open and a long document?
5. **Check each and the everyday-word marks.** Is it clear why "Kiran" is asked about one place at a time and "Priya" is not? Is "also an everyday word" the right words?
6. **The AI dialog.** Is "Found: 1 name from your participant list." plain enough (it counts places, not people: two mentions of one person read "2 names")? Are **Mask** and **Leave** on each "might be a name" obvious?
7. **A characteristics paragraph.** Does Dr. Sharma want Appuscholar to write "n = 5; 3 female; mean age 16" from the list? (Not built; section 11 of the spec.)
8. **The Hindi.** Every Hindi line of the new tab, the plan menu, the Statistics box and the AI dialog is a draft. Does each read as a student would say it? (Known: the Hindi for "withdrew" in the Statistics box reads "took back their name".)
