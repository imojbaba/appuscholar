# Wave D walk-through: editor extras

Definition of done for wave D (numbered captions, the picture editor, the photo studio window, dictation extras, the quick switch on the AI pill, the specialty pack, the writing-sample capture, the resizable AI pane): a student meets each of them on the live site. Tests passing is necessary, not sufficient.
The same behaviour runs automatically in `apps/web/e2e/wave-d.spec.ts` (one test per feature, with the AI and the speech service faked); this sheet is the human walk on the real browser services.

**Site:** https://appuscholar-web.vercel.app
**You need:**
- **A fresh throwaway account** (a new email address you can sign in with), used for nothing else.
- **Your Groq or OpenAI key**, and a passphrase of at least 10 characters for the vault (rows 3, 4 and 9 need the vault on).
- **Chrome or Edge** on a desktop at about 1440 px wide, allowed to open pop-up windows for this site (row 4), with a working microphone (row 5). Firefox and Safari have no dictation; row 5b checks what they say.
- **A photo** on your computer (a JPEG of a few MB) and **a screenshot** (a PNG) for rows 3, 4 and 10. Nothing personal: these pictures go into a test document.

**Record:** fill the last column with `VERIFIED` or `FAILED` and the date (`YYYY-MM-DD`). Anything FAILED is fixed and re-run before wave D is called done. Build under test: Help, the "Version ..." line: `_______`
